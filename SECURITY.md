# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

1. Open the repository's **Security** tab.
2. Choose **Report a vulnerability** and describe what you found and how to reproduce it.

You will get a reply as soon as the maintainers can look at it.

## What is covered

The Kingdom Quiz website and the rules in `database.rules.json` that protect the live audience rooms.
