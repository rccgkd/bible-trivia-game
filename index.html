Before We Begin

How to Play

  1. Turns. Between 1 and 4 players take turns answering Bible trivia questions, one player at a time.
  2. Scoring. A correct answer earns +10 points. A wrong answer costs −5 points. Passing scores 0 points.
  3. Lifelines. Each player may use each of these once per game, whenever they like on their own turn:
    • 50:50 — removes two wrong options.
    • Phone a Friend — 30 seconds to consult someone off-screen.
    • Ask the Audience — everyone connected on their phones votes live, and the results appear as an animated bar chart.
    Once a player uses a lifeline, it's gone for the rest of the game — for that player.
  4. Host control. Only the host advances the game — revealing answers and moving to the next turn.
  5. Winning. After every player completes their set of questions, the player with the highest score wins the crown. 👑
