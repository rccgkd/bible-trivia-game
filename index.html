A host-led Bible trivia game for church game nights — gather round, pick a role, and let's begin.

Quick Training

How to Play

A 60-second walkthrough before we start — here's everything you'll see on screen.

🔄

Turns

1 to 4 players take turns answering, one question at a time, going around the table.

🏆

Scoring

✔ +10 correct · ✘ −5 wrong · ➖ 0 pass

🃏

Lifelines

Each player gets these once per game:

  • ➗ 50:50 — removes two wrong options
  • 📞 Phone a Friend — 30s to ask someone
  • 🙌 Ask the Audience — everyone votes live
🎙️

Host Control

Only the host reveals answers and moves the game to the next turn.

🔔

Score Pop-Up

After every answer, a quick pop-up shows the points earned and the running total — then it's straight to the next turn.

👑

Winning

Once everyone's questions are done, the highest score wins the crown.

Quick Training

Joining as Audience

Here's what happens on your phone during the game.

🔢

Enter the Code

Type the 4-character room code shown on the host's screen.

👀

Watch & Wait

You'll see the current question and whose turn it is — no action needed most of the time.

🙌

When It's Voting Time

If the host uses "Ask the Audience," your options light up — tap the one you believe is right.

🗳️

One Vote Only

Once you tap an answer, it's locked in — watch the results bars fill live on the host's screen.
