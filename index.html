Kingdom Quiz

A host-led Bible trivia game for church game nights. One screen runs the game, and everyone else joins from their phone. Pick a role to begin.

  • 750 questions across the Bible and Church History
  • 1 to 4 players, taking turns
  • Free to play, no account needed

Kingdom Quiz is open source.

How to play

A 60-second walkthrough before we start. Here is everything you will see on screen.

Turns

One to four players take turns answering, one question at a time, going around the table.

Scoring

+10 correct −5 wrong 0 pass

Lifelines

Each player gets these once per game:

  • 50:50 removes two wrong options
  • Phone a Friend gives 30 seconds to ask someone
  • Ask the Audience lets everyone vote live

Host control

Only the host reveals answers and moves the game to the next turn.

Score pop-up

After every answer, a quick pop-up shows the points earned and the running total, then it is straight to the next turn.

Winning

Once everyone’s questions are done, the highest score wins the crown.

Joining as audience

Here is what happens on your phone during the game.

Enter the code

Type the 4-character room code shown on the host’s screen.

Watch and wait

You will see the current question and whose turn it is. No action is needed most of the time.

When it’s voting time

If the host uses “Ask the Audience,” your options light up. Tap the one you believe is right.

One vote only

Once you tap an answer it is locked in. Watch the results fill live on the host’s screen.
